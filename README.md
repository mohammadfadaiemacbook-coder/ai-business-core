# AI Business Core

An AI-assisted business automation project focused on building a sustainable online business.

## Initial Priorities

1. Research and validate English-language YouTube niches.
2. Develop a repeatable workflow for video ideas, research, scripts, and content planning.
3. Support YouTube with Instagram content.
4. Explore Persian-language e-commerce as a later phase.

## Operating Principles

- Prefer free or low-cost tools.
- Test workflows before building a management dashboard.
- Require owner approval before publishing, spending money, creating accounts, or taking irreversible actions.
- Never store passwords, API keys, or private credentials in this repository.

## Project Status

Initial setup. Market research and workflow design are next.

## Planned Structure

- `research/` — niche research and validation
- `youtube/` — ideas, scripts, and publishing plans
- `instagram/` — supporting content
- `automation/` — workflow definitions
- `docs/` — setup instructions and decisions
